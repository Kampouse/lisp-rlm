New session

One signature creates a scoped session key. After this — zero popups.
The key can call only these methods on only this contract, can never attach deposits, and funds structurally cannot leave your account. Revoke anytime.

Deposit to gas pad

Trading capital lives inside the pool contract. This needs your full key — the only popup path.
Gas keys can't attach deposits (protocol rule) — that's what makes them safe. Deposit once, trade popup-free.

Withdraw pad

Drain your gas pad back to your wallet. Requires a 1-yoctoNEAR signature (session keys structurally can't do this).
